# 119. BedTruth — Physical vs Administrative Bed State Reconciliation

**119. BedTruth — Physical vs Administrative Bed State Reconciliation**
**Problem Statement**
A hospital's administrative bed management system, used for capacity planning, can disagree with the real physical occupancy of beds as confirmed by staff or sensors, because updates from either side can lag, and during a genuine capacity crunch this disagreement causes staff to believe a bed is available when it is not, or unavailable when it actually is, without any clinical judgment being involved at all, purely because two independent records of the same physical reality have drifted apart. Design a system that reconciles simulated administrative bed records against physical confirmation signals arriving with realistic delay, and ensures capacity decisions are only made on confirmed-accurate bed state, correctly identifying and resolving cases where the two sources currently disagree. A convincing demonstration should create a genuine disagreement between administrative and physical bed state during simulated high demand, and show the system correctly identifying and resolving it before an incorrect capacity decision is made. Two simulated confirmation sources may disagree and one occupancy event may arrive out of order; the system must represent an explicit unknown state rather than guessing availability and resolve that uncertainty within a declared bound before the bed is assigned. All devices, sensors, tags, transports, and confirmation signals in this challenge are simulated software endpoints or events; no physical medical device, reader, sensor, or vehicle is required.
